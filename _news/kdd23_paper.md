Is there a single "best" algorithm for Learning from Label Proportions? We argue no: the dependence structure between bags, items, and labels defines distinct LLP variants, and accounting for it leads to better model selection across a wide range of datasets and algorithms.

- [Paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3580305.3599307)
- [Code](https://github.com/gaabrielfranco/llp-variants-kdd)
