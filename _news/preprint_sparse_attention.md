Can we identify the key signals that attention heads use to communicate when a model performs a task? This preprint exposes a new phenomenon — sparse attention decomposition — and uses it to trace circuits in GPT-2 small in finer detail than before.

- [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00340)
