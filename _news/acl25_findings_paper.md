Led by **Pedro Calais**, this paper asks whether LLMs treat text and mathematics as one skill or two. By separating the reading step from the equation-solving step, we find a bidimensional structure in model reasoning: small models struggle far more with interpreting the text than with solving the math.

- [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.656/)
